# ev-chargebook — Terms of Use

Personal, self-hosted application operated by a single individual for use
with their own Volvo vehicle. Data is fetched from the Volvo Cars APIs
solely to construct a personal charging-session history, stored on
infrastructure controlled by the operator. No third-party sharing. Not
offered as a service to others. Provided as-is, no warranty.
